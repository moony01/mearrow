## Who can apply

Anyone born in 2008 or later may apply, regardless of gender or nationality. Choose one category: vocal, rap, dance, or other (including instrument, acting, and composition).

## Pre-registration deadline

According to the official page, online pre-registration ran from August 3 through 24:00 KST on August 23, 2026, and is now closed. There is no separate on-site registration. Applicants who passed the first round were to receive the detailed audition time and instructions by email between August 24 and 30.

## In-person audition schedule

The official schedule lists the following locations:

- September 5: Incheon (Jemulpo-gu Youth Center)
- September 6: Boryeong (Boryeong Youth Complex Content Creation Center)
- September 12: Gunsan (Gunsan Youth Center) and Haenam (Haenam-gun Youth Nuri Culture Center)
- September 13: Mokpo (Mokpo Youth Culture Center)
- September 16: Pohang (Pohang Buk-gu Youth Culture House)
- September 20: Geoje (Geoje Youth Center)
- October 4: Sokcho (Sokcho Youth Center)
- October 17: Jeju (Wolpyeong) (Jeju Hoeun Art Center)
- October 18: Seogwipo (Seogwipo Youth Culture House)

Pre-registration through the [official SOURCE MUSIC schedule](https://www.sourcemusic.com/audition/index) has closed. Check the [official application page](https://sourcemusic.hybeaudition.com/en) for the regional schedule and any updates.
