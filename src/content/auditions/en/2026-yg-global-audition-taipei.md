## Taipei audition schedule (pre-application closed)

This listing reflects the 2026 YG GLOBAL AUDITION in Taipei published on YG's official audition page.

- **In-person audition:** October 9, 2026
- **Online pre-application deadline:** October 5, 2026 at 11:59 PM (UTC+8)
- **Audition time and venue notification:** October 7, 2026

Walk-in applications will not be accepted. Only applicants selected from the online pre-application will receive the audition time and venue by email at the address entered in the application. Check the spam or junk folder if the message is not visible.

## Eligibility and categories

Applicants born from 2008 through 2015 may apply regardless of gender or nationality. Choose one category that best showcases your ability:

- Vocal
- Rap
- Dance
- Other, including visual appeal or acting

## How to apply and important notes

The online pre-application closed on October 5, 2026, and the in-person audition was scheduled for October 9. Applicants who were selected through the pre-application should follow the individual email instructions. Each applicant could apply only once across the entire 2026 YG GLOBAL AUDITION schedule.

Application status and schedule details may change. Review the [official YG Taipei audition detail](https://www.yg-audition.com/en/auditions/ongoing/61) again immediately before applying.
