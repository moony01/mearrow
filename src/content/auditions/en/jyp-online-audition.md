## Year-round online applications

JYP Entertainment's online audition is open year-round. Applicants may apply regardless of age, gender, or nationality. Results are sent individually to successful applicants only.

## Categories

Choose the category that best shows your strengths:

- Vocal: any genre is accepted; show your singing fundamentals and technique.
- Dance: original choreography or any dance genre is accepted.
- Rap: covers and original songs are both accepted.
- Acting: prepare a free performance of about one minute.
- Model: prepare a walk and three poses.

## Important notes

An audition submission cannot be edited after it is sent. Review the official application instructions for the country or region selected in the application form before submitting.

Apply through the [official JYP online audition page](https://audition.jype.com/audition/auditions/1). Successful applicants are contacted individually.
