## Bangkok audition schedule

This listing reflects the 2026 YG GLOBAL AUDITION in Bangkok published on YG's official audition pages.

- **In-person audition:** November 7–8, 2026
- **Online pre-application deadline:** October 23, 2026
- **Selected-applicant notification:** October 30, 2026

Only applicants selected from the online pre-application will receive the exact audition time and venue by email at the address entered in the application. Check the spam or junk folder if the message is not visible.

## Eligibility and categories

Applicants born from 2008 through 2015 may apply regardless of gender or nationality. Choose one category that best showcases your ability:

- Vocal
- Rap
- Dance
- Other, including visual appeal or acting

## How to apply

Submit the online pre-application through the official YG Audition website or the QR code in the official notice. Each applicant may apply only once across the entire 2026 YG GLOBAL AUDITION schedule. Make sure your face is clearly visible in every submitted photo and video.

Application status and schedule details may change. Review the [official YG Bangkok audition detail](https://www.yg-audition.com/en/auditions/ongoing/60) and [official notice](https://www.yg-audition.com/en/notice/240) again immediately before applying.
