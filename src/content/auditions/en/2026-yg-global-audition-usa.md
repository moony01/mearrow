## United States audition schedule — completed

This listing reflects the official YG notice for the 2026 U.S. global audition. All three in-person rounds have now concluded:

- **Los Angeles:** October 3, 2026, The LINE LA.
- **Dallas:** October 10, 2026, Korean Cultural Center of Dallas.
- **New Jersey:** October 12, 2026, Korean Community Services.

YG accepted on-site registration from 1:00–4:00 PM on each audition date. Registration is closed. The official notice required a valid photo ID, parental consent or accompaniment for applicants under 18, and a YouTube URL for dance applicants.

## Eligibility and categories

Applicants born from 2008 through 2015 may apply regardless of gender or nationality. Choose one category:

- Vocal
- Rap
- Dance

The [official YG U.S. audition notice](https://www.yg-audition.com/en/notice/244) remains the source for the completed schedule and any follow-up information.
