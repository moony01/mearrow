## United States audition schedule

This listing reflects the official YG notice for the 2026 U.S. global audition. The Los Angeles round took place on October 3; the remaining listed rounds are:

- **Dallas:** October 10, 2026, Korean Cultural Center of Dallas. On-site registration: 1:00–4:00 PM.
- **New Jersey:** October 12, 2026, Korean Community Services. On-site registration: 1:00–4:00 PM.

This round uses on-site registration only. Bring a valid photo ID. Applicants under 18 must bring parent or legal guardian consent or accompaniment as described in the official notice. Dance applicants should prepare a YouTube URL for their performance.

## Eligibility and categories

Applicants born from 2008 through 2015 may apply regardless of gender or nationality. Choose one category:

- Vocal
- Rap
- Dance

Check the [official YG U.S. audition notice](https://www.yg-audition.com/en/notice/244) for venue instructions and updates before attending.
