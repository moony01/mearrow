## Hong Kong audition schedule

This listing reflects the 2026 YG GLOBAL AUDITION in Hong Kong published on YG's official audition page.

- **In-person audition:** October 11, 2026
- **Online pre-application deadline:** October 5, 2026 at 11:59 PM (UTC+8)
- **On-site audition details notification:** October 7, 2026

Walk-in applications will not be accepted. Only applicants selected from the online pre-application will receive the audition time and venue by email at the address entered in the application. Check the spam or junk folder if the message is not visible.

## Eligibility and categories

Applicants born from 2008 through 2015 may apply regardless of gender or nationality. Choose one category that best showcases your ability:

- Vocal
- Rap
- Dance
- Other, including visual appeal or acting

## How to apply and important notes

Submit the online pre-application through the [official YG Audition website](https://www.yg-audition.com/) or the official QR code. Each applicant may apply only once across the entire 2026 YG GLOBAL AUDITION schedule. Make sure your face is clearly visible in every submitted photo and video.

Application status and schedule details may change. Review the [official YG Hong Kong audition detail](https://www.yg-audition.com/en/auditions/ongoing/62) again immediately before applying.
