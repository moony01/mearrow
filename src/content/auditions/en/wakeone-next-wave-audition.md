## Ongoing applications

WAKEONE accepts online audition applications at any time. There are no restrictions on age, gender, or nationality, but applicants must have no impediment to international travel. Applicants may apply for up to two of singing, dancing, rapping, and acting.

## What to submit

- A self-introduction in Korean (foreign nationals should include a self-introduction in their native language) and a front-facing, landscape upper-body headshot.
- A full-body, landscape audition video showing your talent in singing, dancing, rapping, or acting. It should be about two minutes and 30 seconds or less per category, and multiple categories may be selected.
- Contact details other than email, such as an SNS ID, phone number, or messenger ID.

The official FAQ says that the application form, a brief self-introduction video, and a talent video must all be sent for an application to be accepted. Send the completed application and materials to the email shown on the official page. The Korean page lists **wakeone_audition@wake-one.com** under How to apply; the English page also shows **audition@wake-one.com** there, while its FAQ names **wakeone_audition@wake-one.com** as the official audition email. Confirm the receiving address on the [official WAKEONE audition page](https://wake-one.com/en/audition/) immediately before sending.

Results are sent only to selected applicants, usually within about one to three weeks. The official FAQ says applicants may reapply once in the following month, or sooner when updating their materials.
