## Sydney and Auckland audition schedule

This listing reflects the 2026 YG GLOBAL AUDITION notice published by YG Entertainment.

- **Sydney:** December 20, 2026 (Sun), IMI DANCE STUDIO
- **Auckland:** December 22, 2026 (Tue), Auckland Korean Community Centre
- **Online pre-registration opens:** November 2, 2026 at 5:00 PM local time. Further registration details will be announced on that date.

Online pre-registration and on-site registration on the audition day are both available. Applicants choose one category and prepare a performance within one minute.

## Eligibility and categories

Applicants born from 2008 through 2015 may apply regardless of gender or nationality. Choose one category:

- Vocal
- Rap
- Dance
- Other, including visual appeal or acting

Review the [official YG Sydney and Auckland notice](https://www.yg-audition.com/en/notice/246) for registration instructions and any schedule changes before applying.
