## Schedule and status (pre-application closed)

- **Online pre-application deadline:** Sunday, September 6, 2026 at 11:59 PM Japan Standard Time (JST)
- **Audition date:** Sunday, September 20, 2026
- **Time and venue notification date:** Friday, September 11, 2026

Online pre-applications are closed, and walk-in applications are not accepted. Only applicants selected from the pre-application will receive the exact audition time and venue at the email address entered in their application. Applicants should also check their spam or junk folder. The current public page does not publish the exact time or venue.

## Eligibility

Applicants born from 2008 through 2015 may apply, regardless of gender or nationality.

## Categories

Choose **one category** that best demonstrates your ability:

- Vocal
- Rap
- Dance
- Other, including visual or acting

## How to apply and important notes

The process was an online pre-application through the official YG Audition website. See the [official Osaka audition detail](https://www.yg-audition.com/en/auditions/ongoing/58) and [official notice](https://www.yg-audition.com/en/notice/236) for the source text and current status.

- Each person may apply only once across the entire 2026 YG GLOBAL AUDITION schedule.
- The applicant's face must be clearly visible in every submitted photo and video.
- Exact timing and venue details are emailed only to applicants who pass the pre-application.

Schedules and application availability may change. Check the latest official guidance again before relying on this information.
