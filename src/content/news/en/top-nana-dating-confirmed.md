`T.O.P and Nana dating` is no longer a theory built from a music-video frame. On October 2, both artists’ agencies confirmed that the former BIGBANG rapper and the former After School member are in a relationship. The confirmation arrived after a Dispatch report, but the more important detail was not the report itself. It was the timeline: the two met while making T.O.P’s “Studio54” music video, grew closer afterward, and began a romantic relationship around June, according to their agencies and Yonhap’s account of the statements. [Yonhap reported the confirmation](https://en.yna.co.kr/view/AEN20261002005000315), while [CNA documented Nana’s message to fans](https://cnalifestyle.channelnewsasia.com/entertainment/top-nana-dating-korean-stars-589901).

That makes this more than a celebrity-couple headline. It turns a piece of comeback promotion into a small case study in how K-pop stories change meaning after the fact. A project that first arrived as T.O.P’s long-awaited solo return now sits beside a confirmed relationship, a past marked by controversy and distance, and an audience trying to separate what was visible on screen from what was only known privately.

## T.O.P and Nana dating changed the meaning of “Studio54”

### From music-video collaborators to a confirmed relationship

The public already had a shared image of T.O.P and Nana before anyone had a confirmed relationship to discuss. In April, Nana appeared in “Studio54,” one of the double-title tracks from T.O.P’s first full-length solo album, *ANOTHER DIMENSION*. Its theatrical styling made the collaboration feel like a story rather than a simple guest appearance. [SBS described the project](https://sbsstar.net/article/N1008508100/top-nanas-surprise-pairing-studio54-music-video-drops-april-8-with-squid-game-team) as a pairing built around T.O.P’s return and Nana’s performance.

At the time, the safe reading was also the correct one: they were artists working together. The music video could suggest chemistry, but it could not establish a private relationship. The earlier visuals were not retroactive proof; they were professional material that later acquired a second interpretation.

The confirmed timeline is narrower than the internet version of the story. T.O.P’s agency said the two first met through filming, became closer afterward, and developed a romantic relationship around June. Nana’s agency separately acknowledged that they are dating. The statements establish the relationship and its broad timing, but they do not turn every past glance, pose, or promotional appearance into evidence.

Nana’s first reaction also set a useful tone. In a Bubble message, she acknowledged that the announcement had surprised her too and said they would continue seeing each other happily and healthily. It does not ask fans to solve the couple’s private life; it marks the public announcement as the boundary of what is being offered.

## Why the announcement landed harder than a normal dating story

### T.O.P’s return had already made every new image heavier

The timing matters because T.O.P was not returning to a blank public canvas. He debuted with BIGBANG in 2006, later pursued acting, and stepped away from activities after the 2017 marijuana case. Yonhap’s report also notes his return to the spotlight through *Squid Game* Season 2 in December 2024.

His 2026 solo activity had begun to redraw that frame. *ANOTHER DIMENSION* arrived in April, and “Studio54” put him and Nana in the same visual world. Recent profiles have presented the long absence as creative rebuilding rather than a simple marketing pause. In a [recent MK interview](https://www.mk.co.kr/en/musics/12141463), T.O.P described the years away as a time in which he found himself through music. That does not erase the controversy, but it explains why his return is being watched as a personal reset as much as a release campaign.

Nana brings a different kind of continuity. She came from After School and built a parallel acting career. Yonhap’s profile of the couple describes her as an artist who has continued balancing music and acting. Her public image is less about a dramatic comeback than durable visibility: she moves between music, film, television, and fashion without needing a single project to define her.

That contrast is what gives the story its unusual shape. T.O.P’s name carries the tension of a difficult return. Nana’s carries the steadiness of a long-running multi-hyphenate career. Their relationship therefore reads to audiences as both a romance confirmation and a meeting point between two different kinds of public memory.

## How the public sees this revelation

### The additional story attached to T.O.P’s name

The current public response cannot be reduced to one universal mood. Recent coverage shows at least two overlapping readings. One is curious and supportive: T.O.P has returned to music, Nana is a high-profile collaborator, and the couple has now acknowledged a relationship that began after working together. The other is more cautious: discussions of T.O.P still routinely carry the 2017 controversy and the years in which he was largely absent from public activity.

That split is not a contradiction. It is what happens when a public figure returns with new work before the old story has disappeared. Current profile coverage, including [GQ Hong Kong’s interview framing](https://gqhongkong.com/en/highlight-eng/gq-hype-digital-cover%EF%BC%9At-o-p-another-dimension), emphasizes distance, self-reflection, and a gradual return rather than a clean break with the past.

MEARROW’s reading is that the public is not deciding whether T.O.P is “redeemed” or “finished” through this relationship. It is testing whether his present choices can hold more than one truth at once: he can be a working artist rebuilding his career, a person with a difficult public history, and someone entitled to a private relationship. Public sentiment does not determine what happened between January’s music-video shoot and the relationship confirmation. The confirmed timeline and the audience’s feelings about T.O.P are separate layers.

## MEARROW’s take on T.O.P and Nana

### The relationship is personal, but the story is an IP event

The sharpest part of this story is not that fans noticed chemistry. That usually remains a performance reading. The sharper point is that “Studio54” became a bridge between music, acting, and celebrity culture. T.O.P’s album gained a visual entry point, and the October confirmation sent audiences back to the same content with a new question attached.

That is powerful for attention, but it is also where editorial discipline matters. A confirmed relationship should not rewrite every past collaboration as a secret message. The public can discuss how the timeline changes “Studio54” without pretending the music video contained a confession. The couple’s statements provide the boundary: they met through work, grew closer, and are now together.

The business lesson is quieter. In a fandom economy built on access, a private relationship can become a public event before the people involved are ready to narrate it. Nana’s short Bubble message worked because it acknowledged the surprise without turning the announcement into a full-time content stream. T.O.P’s wider comeback story will be stronger if the work remains the center of gravity and the relationship is allowed to exist beside it.

For the broader BIGBANG context, this also adds another layer to the group’s anniversary year. T.O.P is no longer only being discussed through the group he left or the controversies that followed him. He is building a solo chapter, and Nana is now part of the public beginning of that chapter.

## The takeaway

### The confirmation closes one question and opens another

T.O.P and Nana are dating. Their agencies confirmed it, and Nana addressed fans directly. They met through “Studio54,” became closer afterward, and developed a relationship around June. Those are the facts that can be responsibly stated.

Everything beyond that belongs to interpretation. The music video may feel different now, but it does not become a time capsule of private intent. T.O.P’s past still shapes how his return is received, while Nana’s established career gives the pairing its own weight. The next test is not whether the public can decode another clue. It is whether both artists can keep their work, privacy, and new relationship from being flattened into one endless headline.
