The night KISS OF LIFE got their first music show trophy, the timeline did not turn into pure celebration. On April 16, the `KISS OF LIFE first win` headline arrived with applause, re-evaluation, and lingering discomfort all at once. That is why this story is bigger than one trophy. `Who is she`, released on April 6, was not only the group's first Korean comeback in 10 months. It was their first major test of how far they had actually moved back inside public trust after the cultural-insensitivity controversy that reshaped the way many people read their name in 2025. ## The trophy arrived late, but the timing was almost too perfect

### The first win finally came on April 16

According to Soompi and multiple entertainment outlets, KISS OF LIFE took their first-ever music show win on the April 16 episode of `M Countdown` with `Who is she`, scoring 8,884 points. The number matters, but the timing matters more. April was crowded with heavyweight releases, which made it harder for a small-agency girl group to leave the market with a clear "we are still in this fight" signal. That is why this trophy reads less like a fan-service screenshot and more like proof that KISS OF LIFE still belongs inside the competitive lane in 2026.

### Even the title `Who is she` framed the comeback as an identity test

StarNews and the group's showcase comments both point to the same idea: this was not just a Y2K-style dance-pop return. It was a comeback built around the question of what kind of team KISS OF LIFE wanted to look like on stage now. The second single album `Who is she`, released on April 6, is literally titled as a question. Fans were not only listening to the song. They were reading what kind of face the group was trying to present to the public again. That is what made the first win land harder. The trophy looked like a music result, but it also felt like an intermediate score on a reputational test.

## The numbers suggest re-entry, not full restoration

### The video pace and early chart signals were undeniably alive

The comeback did not open quietly. Reports said the `Who is she` music video crossed 30 million views within its first week, while the song quickly found space on major Korean real-time charts and multiple overseas iTunes rankings. Some outlets also reported movement on Billboard-linked global digital sales tracking. That matters. If KISS OF LIFE had been fully forgotten, that early reaction speed would have been difficult to produce. At minimum, the core fandom and the curiosity layer both clicked back in, and this comeback converted those clicks into a trophy.

### But the market is treating this more like a re-entry than a total reversal

Still, it is too early to treat these numbers as total image restoration. This feels less like the kind of broad public explosion that defined the group's `Sticky` era and more like a controlled re-entry into the growth curve. In other words, this first win is strong enough to erase the lazy "they are finished" narrative. It is not strong enough, at least yet, to declare that the shadow of controversy has fully disappeared.

## How the public is receiving this moment

### The 2025 shadow still sits behind the name KISS OF LIFE

Current public perception is clearly split. In April 2025, S2 Entertainment issued an apology after official content triggered a cultural-sensitivity controversy, and that moment permanently changed the lens through which many people viewed the group. A year later, the reaction is still divided. One side argues that the apology and the group's subsequent work make room for musical re-evaluation. Another side believes trust never fully returned. That is why the performance of `Who is she` is being read as more than chart data. It is functioning as a thermometer for how much willingness to accept the group has actually recovered.

### But present popularity and the weight of the past are not the same layer

It is important to draw a hard line here. A warmer response to KISS OF LIFE in 2026 does not erase the discomfort people felt in 2025. On the other hand, the existence of that controversy does not make current achievements unreal. Public perception and the weight of past events operate on different layers. The real work is not choosing one and deleting the other. It is understanding why this comeback is being read in such a complicated way.

## MEARROW's view on KISS OF LIFE's first win

### This is not a forgiveness certificate. It is the market lifting a hold.

MEARROW's read is straightforward. KISS OF LIFE has not been pushed out of the market, and their stage competitiveness is still sellable. But this first win is not the end of the story. It feels more like the market lifting a hold. The fandom moved again, but the broader public has not fully forgotten. That is why the next release now matters even more. If the next comeback delivers a similar conversion rate, the return becomes a trend. If not, today's trophy may remain a survival proof rather than the start of a new dominant phase.

## "They are back" is still a sentence in progress

### The first win opened the door, but it did not finish the argument

`Who is she` did what KISS OF LIFE needed most. It made people click again, talk again, and eventually award them their first trophy. But the bigger question starts now. Can the group survive the same tension one more time, and next time will the public talk about the music before the baggage? KISS OF LIFE is back. The period, however, has not been written yet.
