As of March 5, BLACKPINK's third mini-album 'Deadline' has recorded **1,774,577 copies** in first-week sales. That's the highest first-week figure for any K-pop girl group in history. The previous record held by aespa's 'My World' (2023) was surpassed decisively, and it's roughly 230,000 copies more than BLACKPINK's own 'Born Pink.'

The numbers say one thing — BLACKPINK still sits at the absolute peak of K-pop's girl group hierarchy.

## Day One Rewrote History

### 1.46 Million on Release Day — A Girl Group First

On February 27, 'Deadline' sold **1,461,785 copies** on its first day according to the Hanteo Chart. This is the first time any K-pop girl group has sold 1.46 million copies in a single day. aespa's previous first-day record was obliterated in one stroke.

Numbers like these on day one aren't explained by fandom purchasing power alone. Pre-order momentum, global distribution networks, and the sheer anticipation of a comeback three years and four months in the making all converged.

## Charts — No. 1 Everywhere

### Billboard Global 200

Lead single "JUMP" debuted at **No. 1** on the Billboard Global 200. It peaked at No. 2 on South Korea's Circle Digital Chart and reached No. 28 on the US Billboard Hot 100. For a girl group comeback single, a Hot 100 entry at 28 is a significant achievement.

## 3 Years and 4 Months of Silence — Why Now

'Deadline' is BLACKPINK's first group project in approximately three years and four months since 'Born Pink' in September 2022. Here's what happened in between:

| Period | Event |
|--------|-------|
| 2023 | Re-signed with YG, allowed to establish individual labels |
| 2023-2025 | Jennie (Odd Atelier), Lisa (LLOUD), Rosé (THE BLACK LABEL), Jisoo (BLISSOO) — solo activities |

| 2026.02.27 | 'Deadline' released — group comeback |

When the 2023 re-signing was announced, the dominant industry narrative was that "group activities are effectively over." Each member establishing their own label and focusing on solo careers led many analysts to conclude that a BLACKPINK group comeback was nearly impossible. 'Deadline' obliterated that prediction.
