1,227,986 copies. For a rookie boy group’s second EP, that number looks almost unreasonable. What makes it louder is the timing: CORTIS got there roughly a month before release, and the figure already stands at nearly three times the group’s debut first-week sales of 436,367. `CORTIS GREENGREEN pre-orders` are no longer just a fandom flex. They are becoming a signal for how fast the 2026 rookie market is being repriced.

## CORTIS GREENGREEN pre-orders are abnormal by rookie standards

### 1.22 million before release week

On April 4, distributors YG PLUS and Universal Records announced that CORTIS’s upcoming mini album `GREENGREEN` had reached 1,227,986 stock pre-orders as of April 2. That means the market locked in seven-figure demand well before the album itself arrived.

### The debut benchmark is already behind them

The sharper comparison is not against another headline, but against CORTIS’s own starting point. Their debut mini album `COLOR OUTSIDE THE LINES` posted 436,367 first-week sales earlier this year. `GREENGREEN` has already pushed close to 2.8 times that number in stock pre-orders alone. For most rookies, the second release tests whether the fandom held. For CORTIS, it looks more like the moment the market decided they should be priced like a major franchise.

| Metric | Figure |
|------|------:|
| `GREENGREEN` stock pre-orders | 1,227,986 |
| Debut first-week sales | 436,367 |
| Gap | about 2.8x |

That is the real story. CORTIS is moving out of the "promising rookie" lane and into the territory where labels and investors start talking about scale.

## The rollout makes the number more dangerous

### `REDRED` lands first, then the full EP

The release calendar is built to convert anticipation into momentum. CORTIS will pre-release the lead single `REDRED` on April 20 at 6 p.m. KST, then drop the full `GREENGREEN` EP on May 4 at 6 p.m. KST. That sequence gives the fandom a fresh trigger point before release day and gives the label more time to turn pre-order heat into streaming and chart velocity.

![A futuristic K-pop market scene symbolizing CORTIS GREENGREEN pre-order momentum](/images/news/cortis-greengreen-preorders-1.webp)
_By exploding early, the pre-order curve has already turned this comeback into an event._

### Their debut era already proved global reach

CORTIS reached 100 million Spotify streams with `GO!`, becoming the first act from the 2025 rookie class to hit that mark with a single track. The NBA then positioned the group as the opening-night headliner for NBA Crossover during All-Star 2026, highlighting their Billboard 200 No. 15 debut EP, 100 million first-month streams, and 13 million-plus combined TikTok and Instagram followers. Add their March Lollapalooza Chicago booking, and the picture gets clearer: this is not a domestic-only sales story.

## Why did it explode right now?

### HYBE backing helps, but it does not explain enough

Being a BIGHIT MUSIC group obviously matters. But that alone does not explain why the story kept extending itself. `COLOR OUTSIDE THE LINES` returned to major chart conversations months after release, cumulative Spotify streams crossed 400 million by late February, and now the second EP is pulling pre-order volume that outpaces the debut benchmark before release week even starts.

### The market is buying the next chapter, not just the last song

CORTIS kept adding scenes to its narrative: U.S. sports-event stages, major festival lineups, international media attention, and fans who respond by buying early and heavily. In the rookie market, a one-week viral moment is useful. What really changes valuation is when fans behave as if the next chapter is inevitable. That is what `GREENGREEN` pre-orders look like right now.

## What MEARROW is actually watching

### The rookie baseline just moved

From MEARROW’s perspective, the biggest takeaway is not simply that CORTIS is winning. It is that the rookie boy-group baseline in 2026 is getting more expensive and more global. Seven-figure pre-orders used to be a comeback-week shock. CORTIS is making it look possible a month in advance.

### It is also a warning to trainees and labels

For aspiring trainees, the message is severe but useful: the market no longer rewards debut alone. Teams are now expected to prove streaming scale, live-event potential, and fandom purchasing power almost immediately. For labels, the implication is even clearer. Rookie development cannot end with music-show visibility. The first six months now need to be designed as a multi-market launch.

## May 4 is where the market will decide what this number meant

So far, anticipation has done the heavy lifting. The real test comes next: whether `REDRED` and the full `GREENGREEN` release can convert advance demand into first-week sales, chart stamina, and a longer growth curve. If that happens, CORTIS will stop being discussed as a hot 2025 rookie and start being treated as one of the market-setting acts of 2026.
