A friendship tattoo was supposed to be a content idea. Instead, it became the latest reason fans were asking whether ATEEZ's San and Wooyoung were more than friends.

The moment came during the first episode of the pair's YouTube channel, `9트쫑`, published on July 15. While discussing ideas that could help the channel reach its early view target, Wooyoung suggested getting another friendship tattoo and filming the entire process. San immediately recognized how fans might read the idea and joked that people would think they were dating. He then went further, stating that the two were not dating and were simply very close friends. [The official episode is available on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NDQ8u8ihGzg), while [Sports Kyunghyang's report](https://sports.khan.co.kr/article/202607161445003) documented the exchange the next day.

That distinction matters. This is not a story proving a secret relationship. It is a story about how a highly visible friendship can generate romantic speculation, and how one of the people involved chose to answer it on camera.

## The Joke That Made the Rumor Visible

### An extra friendship tattoo became the hook

The conversation started as a practical programming discussion. San and Wooyoung were brainstorming for `9트쫑`, a channel built around the idea of completing challenges within nine attempts. According to Sports Kyunghyang, the pair set a target of reaching 500,000 views within those attempts and considered an additional friendship tattoo as a possible high-impact idea if the numbers did not arrive quickly.

Wooyoung's proposal was deliberately elaborate. It was not simply a suggestion to get matching ink. He described asking both families for permission, choosing the placement together, designing the artwork, and filming the process from beginning to end. That turned a familiar friendship symbol into a full piece of variety content.

It also created the exact kind of open-ended visual that fandom culture tends to analyze. Two idols already known for their closeness would be discussing a permanent symbol, parental permission, and a shared design while sitting together on their own channel. Even when the intention is comedic, the ingredients are easy to clip and reinterpret.

San understood that instantly. His first response was not a formal statement. It was a reaction to the way the idea could be received: people might think they were actually dating. The joke worked because it acknowledged the interpretation fans were already making.

## San Did Not Leave the Joke Open to Interpretation

### A direct sentence changed the frame

The important part of the episode was not that a dating joke appeared. It was that San did not let the joke become the only version of the story.

After reacting to Wooyoung's plan, San used the moment to clarify the relationship. The Sports Kyunghyang article quoted him saying, in substance, that they were not dating and were simply very close friends. GMA Entertainment also reported the same exchange, including San's explanation that the two were good friends rather than a romantic couple.

That makes the content easy to package but harder to responsibly exaggerate. A headline can mention the rumor because it was the subject of the exchange. It cannot turn the rumor into a confirmed event. The confirmed record is the opposite: a public denial, delivered by one of the people involved, within a playful conversation about friendship content.

## Why Fans Read Friendship as Romance

### The evidence was chemistry, not confirmation

The speculation did not appear from a single photograph or an alleged private message. It grew from the public image of the San–Wooyoung friendship: matching friendship tattoos, long-running chemistry, and a new channel that makes their closeness the central premise of the content.

Sports Kyunghyang described them as 1999-born peers who had already shared the same friendship-tattoo wording. That background explains why the additional-tattoo idea landed as more than a random gag. The joke was attached to an existing symbol, and the audience already understood that symbol as part of the pair's identity.

But a compelling narrative is not the same as evidence of a relationship. The source coverage establishes that fans and observers had raised the possibility, and that San responded to it. It does not establish a romantic relationship. The safest editorial line is therefore to separate three layers:

- **Confirmed:** San and Wooyoung are close friends, have a friendship tattoo, and discussed another one on `9트쫑`.
- **Reported:** Media outlets covered San's on-camera response to dating speculation.
- **Not confirmed:** Any romantic relationship between the two.

That separation is more than a legal precaution. It is the reason the story remains interesting after the denial. The real subject is how quickly friendship becomes a fandom theory when the people involved are comfortable enough to turn their private-feeling chemistry into public entertainment.

### The channel makes closeness the product

The launch of `9트쫑` changes the context around every interaction. On a standard group schedule, a close exchange might be a brief behind-the-scenes moment. On a duo channel, the relationship itself is part of the format. The audience is invited to watch San and Wooyoung plan, argue, improvise, and build new memories together.

That is powerful programming because it gives fans a repeatable reason to return. It also increases the number of moments that can be clipped, translated, and debated. A line about a tattoo can become a short-form hook. A pause can become a reaction meme. ## MEARROW's Take on the Rumor Cycle

### The responsible hook is the correction

Rumor-shaped content does not have to repeat an unverified claim as if it were fact. In this case, the stronger story is the correction itself.

San's response shows how idols can reclaim the frame without turning a playful moment into a confrontation. He did not issue a lengthy statement or treat the audience as an enemy. He recognized the joke, acknowledged how fans might interpret it, and then stated the relationship as he wanted it understood. That combination made the moment entertaining while still putting a boundary around the speculation.

For media and creators, the lesson is simple: the hook is the tension between what fans wondered and what the record actually says. A responsible headline can still create curiosity with words such as `dating rumor`, `friendship tattoo`, and `directly addressed`. The body must then show the audience where the rumor came from, what was said, and what remains unconfirmed.

That approach also respects the people involved. It does not investigate private lives, search for hidden evidence, or treat silence as proof. It stays with public content and a public answer.

San and Wooyoung gave fans more content about their friendship. The extra-tattoo idea created a dating joke, and San used that same moment to say they were not dating and were simply very close friends. That is the story the available record supports.
