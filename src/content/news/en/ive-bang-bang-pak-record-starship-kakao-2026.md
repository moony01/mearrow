On March 7, IVE's pre-release single **'BANG BANG'** reached **916 hourly Perfect All-Kills (PAK)**, surpassing BTS's all-time record of 913 hours. K-pop chart history has been rewritten.

This isn't just a record update. It's **a historic moment where a girl group toppled a boy group's chart fortress**.

## What Is a Perfect All-Kill

A Perfect All-Kill (PAK) means simultaneously holding #1 on all major Korean music streaming platforms — **Melon, Genie, Bugs, FLO, and Vibe** — on the real-time hourly charts. Each hour at #1 across all platforms counts as 1 PAK.

PAK is the hardest chart metric to achieve in K-pop. Fandom streaming alone can't do it — it requires **organic consumption from the general public**. This is why PAK records are considered the most accurate measure of an artist's mainstream appeal.

## Starship-Kakao Alliance's Chart Dominance

IVE is managed by Starship Entertainment. Starship's parent company is **Kakao Entertainment**, which operates **Melon** — Korea's largest music streaming platform by market share.

| Item | Details |

| Parent Company | Kakao Entertainment |
| Owned Platform | Melon (Korea's #1 market share) |

Under Kakao Entertainment's umbrella sit Starship alongside IST (THE BOYZ), EDAM (IU), Antenna (Yoo Jae-suk), and more. Owning Melon — the single most important platform for K-pop chart performance — means the Kakao alliance **controls both chart infrastructure and content**.

Claims that IVE's PAKs are solely due to Melon ownership are oversimplified. PAK requires #1 on **all five platforms simultaneously** — not just Melon. However, analysis consistently points to structural advantages within Kakao's music ecosystem that benefit Starship artists.

## BTS vs IVE — Fandom Power vs Mainstream Appeal

IVE surpassing BTS's PAK record carries deep symbolic weight. BTS commands the largest fandom in K-pop history. But PAK cannot be achieved through fandom power alone.

| Metric | BTS | IVE |
|--------|-----|-----|
| Global Fandom Size | Dominant #1 | Growing |
| Album Sales | All-time tier | Girl group upper tier |
| Korean Music Charts | Powerful (during comebacks) | **Historic (PAK every release)** |

BTS's strength lies in explosive mobilization powered by a global fandom. IVE's strength lies in **penetration into Korea's general public**. Non-fans listen to IVE songs — and that is precisely why PAK happens every single time.
