March 20, 2026. 1:00 PM KST. Three years and nine months of waiting — over.

BTS's fifth studio album **ARIRANG** is out worldwide right now. Named after Korea's iconic traditional folk song, this is the group's first complete studio album after all seven members completed their mandatory military service. The title track **SWIM** music video just dropped and the internet is on fire. Spotify countdown chart #1, 3.45M pre-saves shattered, and tomorrow's Gwanghwamun Square live concert streaming free on Netflix — here's why today is unlike any other day in K-pop history.

## Three Years, Nine Months — What ARIRANG Means

ARIRANG is not just a comeback album. BTS's last studio album *Proof* was released in June 2022. After that, all seven members — RM, Jin, Suga, j-hope, Jimin, V, and Jungkook — fulfilled South Korea's mandatory military service requirement one by one. Big Hit Music made it official on January 4, 2026: BTS would return, and the date was set for March 20.

The album title itself is a strategic statement. Big Hit Music described ARIRANG as "an album that captures BTS's identity as a group that began in Korea." Arirang, Korea's ancient folk song, is sung when parting — and when returning. After nearly four years of silence, there is no more perfect title.

The **SWIM** music video released simultaneously with the album today. Nautical themes, high-energy choreography, and a visual language that immediately sent ARMY into theory overdrive — the MV is everything the teasers promised and more.

## Tomorrow: Gwanghwamun Square — A Historic Comeback Stage

The day after the album release — **Saturday, March 21, 8:00 PM KST** — BTS takes the stage at Seoul's historic **Gwanghwamun Square** for **BTS THE COMEBACK LIVE | ARIRANG**.

This outdoor free concert is ticketed for in-person attendance and simultaneously streamed live on Netflix for all subscribers worldwide. The concert director is **Hamish Hamilton**, best known for directing the Super Bowl Halftime Show.

The choice of venue is deeply symbolic. Gwanghwamun Square sits at the heart of Seoul, steps from Gyeongbokgung Palace. The last time BTS performed near this landmark was in September 2020 for The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon. Five and a half years later, they return to the city's center.
