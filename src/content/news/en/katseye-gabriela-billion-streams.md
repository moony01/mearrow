“Gabriela” crossed 1 billion Spotify streams on Oct. 16, 2026, making it KATSEYE’s first song to enter the platform’s Billions Club. [The Korea Times](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/entertainment/k-pop/20261018/katseyes-gabriela-reaches-1-bil-streams-on-spotify) and [Billboard Brasil](https://billboard.com.br/katseye-1-bilhao-de-streams-gabriela/) both reported the milestone date and first-entry detail.

## The Spotify milestone landed on October 16

The song reached the mark about 16 months after release. Both reports identify Friday, Oct. 16, as the date Spotify’s count passed one billion. Spotify’s official [BILLIONS CLUB playlist](https://open.spotify.com/playlist/37i9dQZF1DX7iB3RCnBnN4) lists “Gabriela” under KATSEYE.

## “Gabriela” was released on June 20, 2025

KATSEYE’s official [Weverse notice](https://weverse.io/katseye/notice/27860) announced June 20, 2025, as the single’s release date. On release day, [Universal Music Canada](https://www.universalmusic.ca/2025/06/20/katseyes-new-single-gabriela-is-out-now/) also described “Gabriela” as out. Billboard Brasil’s report gives the same date.

## KATSEYE’s first song in the Billions Club

Spotify’s playlist records “Gabriela” as a KATSEYE track in the BILLIONS CLUB collection. The Korea Times and Billboard Brasil describe it as the group’s first song to reach the one-billion-stream threshold. Those are the confirmed milestones: a June 20, 2025 release, an Oct. 16, 2026 streaming mark, and KATSEYE’s first entry in Spotify’s club.
