On March 3, Big Hit Music revealed the full tracklist and producer credits for BTS's fifth studio album 'ARIRANG.' When industry insiders read through the credit list, one phrase summed it up: **"This isn't K-pop history — it's pop history."**

Diplo. Kevin Parker (Tame Impala). Flume. JPEGMAFIA. Mike WiLL Made-It. Ryan Tedder. El Guincho. Teezo Touchdown.

These names appearing simultaneously on one album's credits would be impossible for anyone except BTS. The king of EDM, the god of psychedelic pop, the master of experimental electronic music, the icon of hip-hop underground, the mainstream R&B hitmaker, the emotional pop specialist — all operating in completely different musical universes, all converging under a single album on March 20, 2026.

## The Full 14-Track Tracklist

The complete ARIRANG tracklist is as follows:

1. Body to Body
2. Hooligan
3. Aliens
4. FYA
5. 2.0
6. No. 26
7. **SWIM** (lead single)
8. Merry Go Round
9. NORMAL
10–14. (five additional tracks)

The lead single is track 7, 'SWIM.' RM holds writing credits on every track except the interlude, confirming that the album is heavily shaped by lyrics the members wrote themselves during their military service.

## Why Diplo Has 5 Tracks

The most shocking credit belongs to **Diplo**, who is reportedly behind an astonishing **5 tracks** on the album. No single external producer has ever been credited on five BTS tracks before.

Diplo had been teasing this well before the reveal. In a late 2025 podcast interview, he said his collaboration with BTS would "shock the world." The industry took it as hyperbole. The credit sheet proved otherwise.

Diplo's signature sound — spanning EDM, trap, and reggaeton — aligns in some ways with the pop direction BTS pursued before their hiatus, while simultaneously offering the potential to twist it in directions no one expects. Track titles like 'Hooligan' and 'FYA' suggest at least part of this album carries an edge unlike anything BTS has released before.

It's worth noting that Diplo remains a polarizing figure: some international fan communities have raised concerns about past personal controversies in his background, calling on Big Hit Music to address the issue. How HYBE navigates this will be a closely watched storyline going forward.

## Kevin Parker: The Psychedelic Touch on Track 8

**Kevin Parker** is the sole creative force behind Australian band Tame Impala, and a producer who has defined the psychedelic aesthetics of contemporary pop through collaborations with Lady Gaga, The Weeknd, and Mark Ronson. He handles album track 8, **'Merry Go Round.'**

Credits published by Complex and Billboard list Kevin Parker alongside RM, SUGA, j-hope, and Pdogg as co-writers — meaning this track was built in direct creative partnership with BTS members, not simply handed off.

Tatler Asia analyzed the pairing: "If Parker's influence is pronounced, 'Merry Go Round' could layer reverb, live instrumentation, and melodic introspection into BTS's pop framework." Tame Impala's trademark dreamy, retro-tinged sound meeting BTS's emotional depth may well be the album's most surprising highlight.

## Flume, JPEGMAFIA: Pushing the Experimental Edge

**Flume** is the Australian electronic music producer who won a Grammy by defying every convention in sound design. The fact that Flume appears on a BTS album signals this record is exploring sonic territory beyond what most would consider "accessible pop."

**JPEGMAFIA** is an even more striking inclusion. A figure who dismantles boundaries between underground hip-hop, noise, and experimental music, his name on a mainstream K-pop album is itself a genre-redefining event. TopHit called it simply: "ARIRANG will be the most sonically ambitious album in K-pop history."

## Ryan Tedder: Guaranteed Hitmaking

**Ryan Tedder** is the songwriting machine behind Adele's 'Rolling in the Deep' (co-written), hits for Beyoncé, Taylor Swift, and One Direction, and countless other chart-toppers. According to Complex's credits reveal, Tedder co-wrote one track alongside RM, Pdogg, and others.

Ryan Tedder's presence guarantees that no matter how experimental ARIRANG gets, it will never lose the thread of accessibility. He is the symbolic embodiment of Big Hit Music's dual strategy: reach for the experimental and still bring the masses.

## Why They All Said Yes to BTS

That all these producers chose to work with BTS carries meaning beyond a business contract.

Kevin Parker said at the 2025 SXSW panel: "The BTS members know exactly what they want. I found myself learning more from the session than I expected." Flume noted on Instagram that "this was one of the most interesting projects I've ever been a part of." The openness with which Western music's elite discusses working with BTS suggests that the group is no longer perceived as "K-pop stars" in a niche sense — they are recognized as **global creative partners**.

This marks the complete erasure of the "niche pop star" label that Western music circles briefly attached to BTS in the early 2020s.
